# Project Name:
WANAS | ونس

## Project Overview:
WANAS is a digital platform designed to make life easier for students living away from their hometowns. The application helps students find essential services such as accommodation, food, transportation, and other nearby services, all in one place.

## Project Objectives:
- Help students living away from home find essential services easily.
- Simplify the process of finding suitable accommodation.
- Help students discover nearby food options and useful local services.
- Reduce the time and effort spent searching for essential needs.
- Provide a simple and user-friendly experience for students in a new city.

## Project Scope:
- Student registration and profile.
- Accommodation discovery and filtering.
- Food and restaurant discovery.
- Nearby essential services.
- Location-based service discovery.
- Search and filtering.
- Service and accommodation details.
- Reviews and ratings.
- User-friendly navigation and personalized recommendations.

## Project Plan (5 Weeks):
### Week 1 — UX Research
- Conducted competitive analysis to evaluate existing solutions, features, and market gaps.
- Conducted surveys to identify common needs, behaviors, and challenges among students living away from home.
- Conducted user interviews to explore users' experiences, motivations, and pain points in depth.
- Conducted a SWOT analysis to assess the project's internal strengths and weaknesses, as well as external opportunities and threats.
- Prepared stakeholder questions to understand business requirements and project expectations.
- Synthesized the research findings to identify key pain points, user needs, and opportunities for the WANAS platform.

### Week 2 — UX Design
- Created user personas based on the research findings.
- Developed empathy maps to understand users' thoughts, feelings, behaviors, and pain points.
- Created the current-state user journey to identify problems and friction points in the existing experience.
- Designed the improved future-state user journey to show how WANAS can simplify the experience.
- Defined user flows for the main features of the application.
- Developed the information architecture to organize the application's content and services.
- Created wireframes for the main screens and user flows.

### Week 3 — UI Design
- Create the visual identity for WANAS.
- Develop the design system.
- Design the main application screens.
- Apply typography, colors, icons, and components.

### Week 4 — Prototyping & Testing
- Create a high-fidelity interactive prototype.
- Conduct usability testing with target users.
- Collect feedback.
- Identify and improve usability issues.

### Week 5 — Finalization
- Apply the final improvements.
- Complete the interactive prototype.
- Prepare the final case study and presentation.
- Document the project process and results.
